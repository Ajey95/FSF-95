About This Hub

Built for Unique Minds

Both applications were designed with a single philosophy: technology should adapt to the user, not the other way around. Every interaction is calm, predictable, and empowering.

🍃

No timers, no sudden noises. A gentle environment that reduces cognitive load.

🛒 Calm Market

A gamified market where children practice counting money, budgeting, and identifying real objects using their webcam — all at their own pace, with no timers and gentle feedback.

  • 🎮 Interactive shopping game with progressive levels
  • 📷 AI Object Hunter via TensorFlow.js + webcam
  • 📊 Visual progress charts (Recharts)
  • 🧠 5 math learning strategies with practice modes

Communication · Safety · AI Chat

💬 ASD Assistant

A WhatsApp-style chat interface with embedded AI that decodes sarcasm, emotional tone, and safety risks in real time — helping ASD users navigate digital conversations confidently.

  • 🔍 Real-time sarcasm & emotion detection
  • ☁️ Cloud (Gemini 2.5 Flash) + offline NLP fallback
  • 🛡️ Safety flags for manipulative language
  • ⚙️ Customisable AI persona & sensitivity settings
